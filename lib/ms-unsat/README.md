# MS-unsat

`python -m scripts.setup` installs MS-unsat here, under `src/`, from the
statically linked binaries its authors entered in the Unsolvability IPC 2016 as
MS-unsat-irr (team4v2). It is not tracked in this repository.

MS-unsat is Álvaro Torralba and Jörg Hoffmann's merge-and-shrink planner for
proving unsolvability, built on Fast Downward and distributed under the GNU GPL.
Its source and binaries are available from the
[competition repository](https://github.com/AI-Planning/unsolve-ipc-2016/tree/master/planners/team4v2).
